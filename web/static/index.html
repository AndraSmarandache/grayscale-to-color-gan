Step 1 — LAB color space

The model works in LAB color space. L (luminance) is the grayscale input. The model predicts only the a and b color channels, which are then combined with L to reconstruct the full color image.

L channel (input) Luminance — exactly what a grayscale image contains
Predicted a channel Green ↔ Red axis
Predicted b channel Blue ↔ Yellow axis
Final colorization L + predicted ab → RGB

Step 2 — What the encoder sees

At each stage the ResNet-18 encoder compresses the image spatially while extracting increasingly abstract patterns. Each small image below is one channel of the feature map at that stage — a learned detector responding to something in the input. Early layers fire on edges; later layers respond to semantic concepts like "sky" or "face."

Step 3 — Where is the model uncertain?

The model predicts not just a color but also its confidence in that prediction. Red = high uncertainty (walls, metal, shadows — ambiguous colors). Blue = high confidence (sky, grass, skin — colors the model has seen many times).
